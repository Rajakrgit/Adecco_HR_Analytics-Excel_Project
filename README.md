# Adecco_HR_Analytics-Excel_Project
# 1. Project Overview

The Adecco HR Analytics project analyses employee data to understand
workforce attrition and identify areas where the organisation may
improve employee retention and job satisfaction.

The business case describes Adecco India as experiencing employee
turnover, with particular concern about junior-level employees and the
Sales department. The analysis uses employee-level HR data to examine
attrition across departments, job roles, overtime, business travel,
gender, marital status, job involvement, training, and other workforce
characteristics.

# 2. Business Requirements
• Calculate overall attrition rate.

• Analyse department and job-role performance.

• Compare overtime and business travel.

• Understand employee satisfaction.

• Identify employee retention opportunities.

# 3. Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel: Data analysis and dashboard development.

Techniques: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, conditional formatting and data visualisation.

Functions: COUNTIF, COUNTA, AVERAGE, CORREL and calculated fields.

# 4. Challenges Faced
• Understanding HR metrics and attrition calculations.

• Comparing attrition across departments and job roles.

• Organising multiple Pivot Tables.

• Presenting complex information in one dashboard.

• Interpreting workforce patterns without assuming causation.

# 5. Key Business Insights
• Sales department attrition: 20.63%.

• Sales Representative attrition: 39.76%.

• Overtime employees attrition: 30.53%.

• Non-overtime employees attrition: 10.44%.

• Frequent travellers attrition: 24.91%.

• Non-travellers attrition: 8.00%.

# 6. Recommendations
• Review Sales Representative retention strategies.

• Monitor overtime and employee workload.

• Improve business travel planning.

• Strengthen employee engagement and career development.

• Conduct exit interviews to understand reasons for leaving.

• Monitor attrition trends regularly.
